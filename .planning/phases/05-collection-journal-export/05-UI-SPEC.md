## Typography

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height |
|------|------|--------|-------------|
| Body | 16px | 400 (regular) | 1.5 |
| Label | 14px | 600 (semibold) | 1.4 |
| Heading | 20px | 600 (semibold) | 1.2 |
| Display | 28px | 600 (semibold) | 1.2 |

Hierarchy Rules:
- Display (28px semibold) is reserved for page hero headlines ("Life List", "Field Journal", "Expedition Log").
- Heading (20px semibold) is used for section headers, card titles, and modal headers.
- Label (14px semibold) is used for badges, category pills, buttons, and telemetry stat names.
- Body (16px regular) in Noto Serif is used for field observations, Wikipedia excerpts, and descriptive empty states.

## Visual Hierarchy & Focal Points

- **/collection Focal Point:** Daily Species Hunt hero card anchored at the top of the viewport with vivid progress pips, followed immediately by the horizontal category filter pill carousel.
- **/journal Focal Point:** Rolling 12-week activity heatmap card showing current streak and total active expeditions, anchoring the top of the chronological timeline.
- **/journal/[walkId] Focal Point:** MapLibre interactive header map displaying the glowing polyline trail and color-coded organism and audio pin drops.
- **/settings Focal Point:** Prominent "Export Journal ZIP" card under Storage & Backups, visually balanced against the bounded red "Danger Zone" card at the page bottom.
- **Accessibility Fallbacks:** All icon-only buttons (such as search clear button, audio note play button, and navigation arrows) provide visually hidden `aria-label` text.
